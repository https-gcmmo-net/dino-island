Privacy Policy

Dino Island: Raise & Care · Global Connect Technology · Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the iOS game “Dino Island: Raise & Care” (the “App”) handles. The App is published by Global Connect Technology (“we”, “us”).

1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The App does not collect your location, precise or approximate.
  • The App shows no ads and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress (your dinosaur and its care status, TC balance, items, and settings such as sound and vibration) in local storage on your device. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers. Deleting the App deletes this data.

The App reads your device clock and time zone only on the device, for example to show day and night and to reset monthly game limits. This information is not sent anywhere.

3. Location

The App does not request location permission and does not collect precise or approximate location, including location derived from your IP address.

4. In-app purchases

The App offers optional consumable TC packs (virtual game currency with no cash value). Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment card details. When you buy a pack, the App receives the purchase result from Apple (such as the product and a transaction identifier) and records the delivered TC on your device so that it is not credited twice. Apple’s handling of your purchase is covered by Apple’s own privacy policy.

The App contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service of ours. To offer and deliver in-app purchases the App uses Apple StoreKit through the Unity In-App Purchasing library. For that purpose Apple, and Unity Technologies as the provider of that library, may process purchase history, your interaction with the in-game store, an app or device identifier and basic diagnostic data. We use this information only to deliver and verify purchases and to keep the App working; we do not sell it and do not use it to track you.

5. Network use

The App connects to the internet to complete in-app purchases with Apple and may load game content files (for example language and 3D content). These requests do not include personal information.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules local reminders on your device (for example, that your dinosaur is hungry). They are created on the device; no notification token is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in the iOS Settings app.

7. Children

The App is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Sharing and sale

We do not sell your information and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

9. Your choices and deletion

Because game data is stored on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the App. If you have questions about your information, contact us and we will reply within 30 days.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and its effective date.
