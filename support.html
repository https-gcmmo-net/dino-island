About the game

Hatch a T-Rex egg and care for your dinosaur as it grows: feed it, keep it clean and check its condition. Your progress is saved on your device.

Frequently asked questions

My progress is gone. Can you restore it?

Game progress is stored only on your device. We do not keep a copy on our servers, so deleting the app removes your progress and we cannot restore it.

What is TC?

TC is virtual game currency with no cash value. TC packs are optional consumable in-app purchases, not subscriptions. Purchases require an internet connection.

I bought a TC pack but did not receive it.

Make sure you are online, then close and reopen the game so it can finish the purchase with the App Store. If the TC still does not arrive, email us with the date of purchase and the pack name. Delivered consumable TC is not credited again by Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

All payments are handled by Apple. You can request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the game show the dinosaur eating live prey?

Yes. As the dinosaur grows it is fed live prey (goat, pig, deer). There is no blood and no death scene. The game is intended for ages 13 and up.

How do I turn off sound, vibration or reminders?

Open Settings inside the game. Reminder notifications can also be turned off in the iOS Settings app under Notifications.
